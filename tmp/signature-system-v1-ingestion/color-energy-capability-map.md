# Color and energy capability map

- Every Signature System v1 record declares `tintMode: none`.
- Every Signature System v1 record declares `energyMode: fixed`.
- No global tint, `hue-rotate`, reconstructed glow, or destructive color control is present.
- The Electric Rift Stage and Electric Energy Divider keep their authored raster energy and are tagged `energy-control-pending` until approved fidelity-preserving derivatives exist.
- Environmental cobalt light remains part of the supplied masters; Studio does not recolor the copper, lacquer, or glow.
