6. Concept stills + locked product rules

Atmosphere target — Daniel-level richness, cleaner system. Stills inspire; the CSS assembly above is the editable-part truth.

Lock for this Signature family

  • Studio-owned parts. Pounded Copper rim, Copper Leaves, Bright Lacquer finish, Icon Station — belong to Studio Visual Parts, not “the Button.”
  • Finish ≠ Color. Bright Lacquer is the finish recipe; Host recolors lacquer without swapping the finish socket.
  • Curated → Customize. Selecting this family must open the same sockets used to construct it — never a flattened image.
  • Generic Button survives. Campaigns, coupons, forms, badges, and plain buttons keep Foundation primitives. Signature is additive wow.
  • One material authority. No parallel lacquer/copper renderer. Future Cabinet inventory mounts into these sockets.
  • Compatible reuse. Rim/leaves/finish may later dress medallions, container edges, dividers, underlines, offer frames, Action Surfaces.
  • FREE sourcing only. This target is TapConnect-original construction. Third-party free code/assets require license + provenance if introduced later.
  • Benchmark. Match or exceed Monkey Cage / BreadBreakers tactile richness — with responsive balance and editable parts.

7. Open refinements for Product Owner

Not blocking Cody’s Cabinet Handles. Decide these while the visual target is warm.

  • Medallion default: Always Black Lacquer disc, or medallion follows body lacquer at a darker step?
  • Leaves density: CTA only vs also social? Recommend CTA + Action Surface + Divider terminals; utility stays clean.
